# Design System: Solution Club Wall Printer
**Project ID:** 12472053669639830736

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Aesthetic: Clean, Authoritative, Premium, Trustworthy. The design uses a dark-and-light contrast. The Deep Midnight Navy provides a strong, high-end authority for headers and the hero section, while the Soft Cloud background and Pure White cards keep the content airy and easy to read. Warm Teal accents draw the eye to interactive elements without being overly aggressive.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Deep Midnight Navy (#1B2A4A)** — Primary brand color. Used for the prominent header, hero section background, and authoritative typography. 
- **Warm Teal (#2A9D8F)** — Primary action color. Used for all primary CTA buttons and interactive highlights.
- **Rich Amber (#E9C46A)** — Secondary accent. Used selectively to highlight the engineering/preparation guarantee section and differentiate it from the core printing service.
- **Soft Cloud (#F8F9FA)** — Global page background, delivering a softer contrast than stark white.
- **Pure White (#FFFFFF)** — Surface color for all cards (including the Bento grid) and form inputs.
- **Charcoal (#2D3436)** — Primary body text color. Highly readable on light backgrounds.
- **Steel Gray (#6C757D)** — Secondary text for captions, small print, and labels.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Headings:** 'Inter', Bold (700) with tightened letter-spacing (-0.02em) to ensure a premium, modern, and compact feel.
- **Body:** 'Inter', Regular (400) or Medium (500) for high legibility. Line heights are tall enough to feel "airy".
- **Buttons / Actions:** Bold/Semi-bold to provide clear visual affordance.

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Buttons:** Generously rounded (`border-radius: 12px`), with a minimum height of 48px to ensure they are tap-friendly on mobile and substantial on desktop. Background in Warm Teal.
- **Cards/Containers:** Gently rounded corners (`border-radius: 16px`). Pure White surfaces placed on the Soft Cloud background. They feature a whisper-soft diffused shadow (`0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`) to lift them cleanly off the page.
- **Inputs/Forms:** Clean borders with ample internal padding, pure white backgrounds, and a subtle focus ring.
- **Bento Grid:** Used for the Use Cases section. Snug grouping of uniform cards to present categorized information neatly.

## 5. Layout Principles
- **Grid/Spacing:** Centered max-width containers for all textual content. Ample vertical padding (e.g., `py-16` or `py-24` in Tailwind terms) between major sections to maintain a premium, uncrowded feel.
- **Section Contrast:** Strong alternating contrast between the dark hero and footer sections against the light content sections builds a solid visual rhythm.
